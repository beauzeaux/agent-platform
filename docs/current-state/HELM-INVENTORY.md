Workloads in each row reside in the release namespace. Additional scope:

- `kube-prom-stack` also installs monitoring Services/Endpoints in `kube-system`,
  plus cluster RBAC/webhooks. Its two StatefulSets are generated by the operator

  RBAC. Headlamp's separate viewer RBAC is applied outside its Helm release.
- CRD release records residing in a namespace do not make those CRDs
  namespace-local.
